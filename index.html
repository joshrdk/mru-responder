MRU Responder

Everything here runs: go on duty, claim a shift, log hours, dispatch a call to yourself, file a post-shift report, and approve a new member. The dashboard on the right updates as you go.

1 · Go on dutyThe switch on the home screen turns location sharing on.
2 · Claim Saturday's football slotShifts tab. Crew-leader slots stay locked.
3 · Dispatch a callLeadership view, red button. Accept it on the phone.
4 · Check in, then check outA one-tap vehicle check, then hours post and the report form opens.

Responder view · Josh de Kloet, EMT

A concept prototype for the FSU Medical Response Unit, not an official unit tool. Names, events and figures are made up. Nothing is saved: a refresh starts over, and no real location is ever read.
